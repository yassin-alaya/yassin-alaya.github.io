The Divide-and-Conquer Estimator for High-Dimensional Linear Regression under Weak Exogeneity Job market paper

Working paper. Last revised 6 September 2026.

Abstract

Under weak exogeneity, ordinary least squares is inconsistent when the number of regressors is proportional to the sample size and regressors are autocorrelated, as shown by Mikusheva and Sølvsten (2025). Their consistent estimator relies on homoskedasticity. I propose the Divide-and-Conquer (DaC) estimator, which is consistent under heteroskedasticity when feedback from the error term to future regressors has a known, finite horizon. The idea is simple: partition the sample into subsamples, spaced so that strict exogeneity holds within each, estimate OLS on every subsample, and average. The resulting estimator is unbiased and consistent for linear contrasts of the slope in the proportional regime. Asymptotic normality and consistent variance estimation are established when the number of regressors grows but remains a vanishing fraction of the sample size. Simulations compare DaC, OLS, and the Mikusheva–Sølvsten estimator.
