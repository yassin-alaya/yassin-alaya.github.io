About

I am a PhD candidate at the Toulouse School of Economics working in econometric theory.

My research focus is on high-dimensional econometrics. My job market paper studies time series regression models with many regressors and weak exogeneity. Previously, I have worked in theoretical political economy.

Research interests

  • Many-regressor asymptotics
  • Machine learning methods for econometric inference
  • Time series regression models with weak exogeneity
